Privacy Policy

Site Time Tracker · Last updated: November 10, 2026

Site Time Tracker is a browser extension for Firefox, Brave, and other Chromium-based browsers. It shows you how much time you spend on each website. This policy explains what the extension stores, where it is stored, and the choices you have.

The short version

Everything the extension records stays on your device. We do not collect, receive, sell, or share any of your data. The extension has no servers, accounts, analytics, or advertising.

What the extension records

While you browse, the extension records:

  • The domain name of the site you are actively viewing (for example, example.com). It does not record full page addresses, page titles, page content, searches, form entries, or passwords.
  • The number of seconds you spend on each domain per day.

Time is counted only while the browser window is in focus, the tab is active, and you are not idle. Private or incognito windows are never tracked. Browser pages such as settings or new-tab pages are ignored.

Where the data is stored

The data is saved in your browser's local extension storage on your own device. It is never uploaded or transmitted anywhere. Daily records older than 90 days are deleted automatically.

Why the extension needs its permissions

  • tabs: to read the domain of the tab you are currently viewing.
  • storage: to save your usage data on your device.
  • alarms: to save your progress once a minute.
  • idle: to pause counting when you step away from your computer.

Data sharing

We do not share, sell, or transfer user data to any third party. We do not use or transfer user data for purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose of showing you your own browsing time, and we do not use it for advertising, credit decisions, or lending.

Remote code and third parties

The extension does not load or run remote code and does not include third-party libraries, trackers, or analytics.

Your choices

  • Open the extension popup and choose Clear all data to delete everything it has recorded.
  • Disable or remove the extension at any time. Removing it deletes its stored data from your browser.

Children's privacy

The extension does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new “Last updated” date. If a change affects how data is handled, it will also be noted in the extension's release notes.
